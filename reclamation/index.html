The 14-Day Reclamation

Pattern test

Every quit attempt that didn't stick had the same design flaw.

Willpower removes the tool. It doesn't address what the tool was doing. This 60-second test identifies the specific regulation function nicotine was serving — and what replaces it precisely.

You've stopped before. Maybe more than once. Each time you held it together until something happened — a bad day, a social situation, a moment where the gap became too loud. You didn't fail. The approach was incomplete.
The frustrating part isn't the craving. It's that the craving makes sense. Your nervous system learned to call for a specific tool that actually worked. Removing the tool without replacing the function is why willpower alone doesn't hold.
The regulation function is still running. It just needs a more precise instrument. Five questions identifies which one.

5 questions · 60 seconds · No signup required

When does the craving arrive most reliably?

Not when it's strongest — when it's most predictable.

Question 2 of 5

Question 3 of 5

Set aside the nicotine itself. What was the actual function?

Question 4 of 5

Question 5 of 5

Not the goal — the internal experience of having arrived at it.
