The 14-Day Reclamation

Pattern test

Every quit attempt that didn't stick had the same design flaw.

Willpower removes the tool. It doesn't address what the tool was doing. This 60-second test identifies the specific regulation function nicotine was serving — and what replaces it precisely.

5 questions · 60 seconds · No signup required

Question 1 of 5

When does the craving arrive most reliably?

Not when it's strongest — when it's most predictable.

Question 2 of 5

What does the craving feel like in the body?

Before the thought "I want a cigarette" — what is the physical signal?

Question 3 of 5

What did the habit give you that nothing else did?

Set aside the nicotine itself. What was the actual function?

Question 4 of 5

What happened the last time you tried to stop?

If this is your first attempt, choose the outcome that feels most likely.

Question 5 of 5

What would a successful outcome actually feel like?

Not the goal — the internal experience of having arrived at it.
